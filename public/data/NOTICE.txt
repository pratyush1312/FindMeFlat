The user's private notes, quotes, attachments and preferences are maintained separately and are not part of this public registry database.

Research additions (24 November 2026):
research-0.json through research-9.json are safe-field adaptations of the same Vonter Karnataka RERA CSV snapshot (7 September 2026), ODbL 1.0. No bank accounts, PANs or personal signatory/contact fields retained.
places.json and location-ratings.json are derived from OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. Individual layer dates and incomplete extraction coverage are recorded in places.json.
Metro station/line data adapted from Vonter/bmrcl-gtfs geojson/export.geojson (ODbL 1.0); source commit/date recorded in the dataset documentation. This is not an official BMRCL service feed.
MapLibre GL JS (BSD-3-Clause), Leaflet (BSD-2-Clause), OpenFreeMap/OpenMapTiles/OSM attribution displayed on maps. No tiles are bundled or bulk downloaded.
